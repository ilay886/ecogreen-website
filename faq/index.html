Straight answers about estimates, roofs, siding, gutters, windows and doors from EcoGreen Roofing And Exteriors.

Roofing

Do I need a roof repair or a full replacement?

If the damage is in one area and the rest of the roof is in good shape, a repair usually makes sense. If shingles are curling or cracking across the roof, you see leaks in more than one spot, or the roof is near the end of its life, replacing it often costs less over time. After the inspection we show you photos and explain both options so you can decide.

What are the signs my roof needs attention?

Watch for missing, curling or cracked shingles, granules collecting in the gutters, water stains on ceilings, damp spots or daylight in the attic, and loose flashing around chimneys, vents or skylights. If you notice any of these, call us for an inspection before a small problem turns into a bigger one.

How long does a new roof take?

Most single-family homes take one to two days. Larger or steeper roofs, extra layers to remove or rain can add time. Your estimate includes a timeline, and we keep you posted if the weather moves the schedule.

What roofing materials do you install?

We install architectural asphalt shingles, standing seam metal and flat roof membranes, and we repair slate and cedar shake roofs. We help you pick the right material for your home, your budget and how long you plan to stay.

My roof was damaged in a storm. What should I do first?

Make sure everyone is safe, then take photos of any damage you can see from the ground and inside the house. Call us: we can tarp the roof to stop more water from getting in, inspect it and document the damage with photos. If you file an insurance claim, we can meet your adjuster on site.

Will you help with my insurance claim?

Yes. We inspect the roof, document the storm damage with photos and can meet your adjuster at the house. We also explain what the claim covers so you know what to expect before work starts.

Siding, Gutters, Windows & Doors

Do you only do roofs?

No. Along with roofing, we install and repair siding, seamless gutters and downspouts, leaf guards, windows, doors, garage doors, and fascia and soffit. Many homeowners do two projects at once, such as a new roof and new gutters.

Can you match my existing siding?

In most cases, yes. We repair damaged sections and match the color and profile as closely as possible. When an exact match is no longer made, we explain your options, such as re-siding one full side of the house so the difference does not show.

Are leaf guards worth it?

If you have trees near your home, leaf guards can save you from frequent gutter cleanings and overflowing gutters. We tell you honestly whether your home would benefit.

Should I replace my gutters when I get a new roof?

It is often a good time to do both. Our crews can replace rotted fascia, add drip edge and make sure the new roof and gutters work together to move water away from your home.
