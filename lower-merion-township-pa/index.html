Local Roofing Experts in Lower Merion Township, PA

Lower Merion Township has some of the finest older homes on the Main Line, from stone colonials and Tudors to Victorian twins, and every one of them deserves attention to detail.

Tudors have steep gables and lots of valleys, colonials have big chimneys and Victorians have turrets and porches. We repair slate and cedar where it's sound, rebuild valleys and seal chimneys properly. When a new roof is the right call, we install premium shingles or standing seam metal, plus a flat rubber roof on porches and sunrooms. We help with storm claims too.

Around the house, we hang wide 6-inch seamless gutters and new downspouts, replace soffit and fascia and install James Hardie style siding. Ask us about new windows that cut drafts and a handsome new entry door. Want a quick refresh? Siding can get a soft wash from us with plant-safe products.

Careful work comes from 25+ years of experience on our team, and we're licensed and insured. Lower Merion homeowners work with our Bala Cynwyd team, right in the township, while our Huntingdon Valley and Doylestown branches reach the rest of Montgomery County.
