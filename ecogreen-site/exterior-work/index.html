Exterior Work Questions, Answered

Can one company really handle every part of my exterior?

Yes. Our crews cover roofing, siding, gutters, windows, doors, masonry, stucco, decks and hardscaping. One team means one schedule, one estimate and one point of contact, so nothing falls between contractors.

Do I need a permit for a deck, patio or retaining wall?

Many townships require one, especially for decks and taller retaining walls. We check with your township or borough and handle the permit as part of the job.

Can you fix drainage problems in my yard?

Yes. We look at where water comes off the roof and how it moves across the yard, then fix it with downspout extensions, grading, drains or waterproofing so it moves away from your foundation.

How do I plan several exterior projects at once?

Tell us everything you are thinking about, and we will put it in one written estimate with a sensible order, so the roof goes on before the gutters and the drainage is done before the patio.

Do you offer free estimates for exterior work?

Yes. Every project starts with a free on-site visit and a clear written quote, with no pressure to go ahead.
