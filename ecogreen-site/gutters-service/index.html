Signs Your Gutters Need Attention

  • Water spilling over the edge in rain
  • Gutters pulling away from the house
  • Leaking seams or corners
  • Peeling paint or rot on the fascia
  • Puddles or erosion near the foundation
  • Water in the basement after storms
  • Plants growing in the gutters
  • Rust or holes in old gutters

Gutter Questions, Answered

5-inch or 6-inch gutters?

5-inch gutters suit most homes. We recommend 6-inch for large or steep roofs and homes surrounded by trees, because they carry more water and clog less often.

Are leaf guards worth it?

If you have trees near your home, leaf guards can save you from frequent cleanings and overflowing gutters. We’ll tell you honestly if your home would benefit.

Why do my gutters overflow even when they are clean?

Usually the gutters aren’t sloped right, the downspouts are too few or too small, or the roof sends more water than the gutters can handle. We can re-pitch or upsize them to fix it.

Can you install gutters with a new roof?

Yes. Doing both together is a good time to replace fascia, add drip edge and make sure the new roof and gutters work as one system.

How often should gutters be cleaned?

Most homes need cleaning at least twice a year, in late spring and late fall. Homes surrounded by tall trees may need it more often. Leaf guards can cut that down a lot.

What color gutters can I get?

Seamless aluminum gutters come in many colors, so you can match your trim, fascia or siding. We bring color samples to your estimate.
