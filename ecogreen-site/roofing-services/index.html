Roof Replacement

Signs You Need Roof Repair or Replacement

  • Curling, cracked or missing shingles
  • Shingle granules collecting in your gutters
  • Water stains on ceilings or upstairs walls
  • Daylight or damp spots in the attic
  • A roof line that looks wavy or saggy
  • Moss, algae or soft spots on the roof
  • Ice dams along the roof edge in winter
  • Your roof is 20 or more years old

Not sure? We’ll take a look for free and tell you honestly what your home needs.

Roofing Questions, Answered

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most single-family homes take one to two days, depending on the size of the roof, how many layers come off and the weather. We’ll give you a timeline with your estimate and keep you updated if rain moves the schedule.

Can you put new shingles over my old roof?

We recommend a full tear-off. Removing the old layers lets us check the boards underneath, replace any rot and install ice and water shield properly. It also keeps the manufacturer warranty valid in most cases.

Do you help with insurance claims?

Yes. After a storm we inspect the roof, take photos of the damage, and can meet your adjuster on site. We help you understand what the claim covers so there are no surprises.

Do I need to be home during the work?

No. We just need access to the outside of the house and an outdoor outlet. Most homeowners go about their day, and we check in with you at the start and the end.

What warranty do I get?

Every roof comes with our workmanship warranty, plus the shingle manufacturer’s warranty on the materials. We go over both with you before the job starts.

How much does a new roof cost?

It depends on the size and pitch of your roof, the material you choose, how many old layers come off and how much decking needs replacing. We give you one clear written price after a free inspection, so you know the full cost before anything starts.
