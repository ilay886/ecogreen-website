Local Roofing Experts in Langhorne Manor Borough, PA

Langhorne Manor is a small, quiet borough with big old trees and many older homes. Those trees are part of its charm, and they're also behind most of the calls we get here.

Limbs come down in storms and crack shingles, branches rub roofs bare, and shade keeps the north side damp until moss lifts the edges. When a limb hits, we tarp the roof right away and fix the leak. If the roof is worn out, we'll give you a new one built to last, with a ridge vent so the attic can breathe. After a storm, we can also help with your insurance claim.

Leaves fill gutters fast in Langhorne Manor, so we hang new seamless gutters topped with leaf guards and fix any that sag. We repair wood siding, replace rotted soffit and fascia, and wrap trim in aluminum. Inside, energy-efficient double-hung windows and a sturdy front door make an older home cozy again. Our soft wash clears moss and is safe for both pets and gardens.

Licensed and insured, we look after Bucks County homes with well over 25 years of experience on our side. Our Doylestown location handles your area, with two more offices in Huntingdon Valley and Bala Cynwyd. Estimates are free and pressure-free at (888) 494-1248. We'd love to meet you.
