Local Roofing Experts in Hainesport, NJ

Hainesport sits along the winding Rancocas Creek, and the trees and moisture near the water can be hard on your siding. Faded, cracked or green siding is one of the most common things we see in town, and we know when a panel can be saved and when a whole wall should be redone.

Sometimes just a few pieces need fixing, so we match your existing siding and replace only what's damaged. If it's time for something new, we'll install insulated vinyl or fiber cement that holds up to the damp. We'll also replace rotted soffit and fascia, and wrap the window and door trim in aluminum for a clean finish.

The roof matters too. We fix leaks, redo flashing around chimneys and vents, and install new roofs with architectural shingles and good attic vents. Our seamless gutters with leaf guards keep creekside leaves from piling up. We also install energy-efficient sliding windows, new entry doors and sliding patio doors. For green siding, a gentle soft wash often does the trick.

Licensed and insured, our people carry 25+ years of experience, and you'll get a free estimate without any pressure. Our Huntingdon Valley base is the nearest of three, along with Doylestown and Bala Cynwyd, and serves Hainesport and all of Burlington County. Give us a ring at (888) 494-1248.
