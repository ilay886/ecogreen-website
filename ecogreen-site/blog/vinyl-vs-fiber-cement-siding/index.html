Vinyl vs. Fiber Cement Siding: Which Is Right for Your Home?

Siding · 4 min read · EcoGreen Roofing And Exteriors team

Vinyl and fiber cement are the two most popular siding choices around Philadelphia and South Jersey. Both can look great. They differ in cost, upkeep and how they handle impact.

Vinyl siding

  • Usually the lower upfront cost.
  • Never needs painting; the color goes all the way through.
  • Light and quick to install.
  • Insulated vinyl adds a foam backing for a sturdier feel and better energy performance.
  • Can crack from a hard impact in very cold weather, and very dark colors can fade over time.

Fiber cement siding

  • Made from cement, sand and wood fibers, so it's very rigid and resists impact, rot, insects and fire.
  • Looks the most like real wood, which suits historic and higher-end homes.
  • Costs more and takes longer to install.
  • Needs repainting every so often, though factory finishes last many years.

How to choose. If budget and low upkeep come first, vinyl is hard to beat. If you want the look of wood, plan to stay for a long time, or have an older home with detailed trim, fiber cement is often worth the extra cost.

Either way, good installation matters as much as the material: proper house wrap, flashing around windows and doors, and solid trim work. We're happy to bring samples of both to your free estimate.
