Signs It’s Time for New Siding

  • Cracked, warped or loose panels
  • Soft or rotted spots under the siding
  • Faded color that washing won’t fix
  • Peeling paint or wallpaper inside
  • Mold or mildew coming through seams
  • Higher heating and cooling bills
  • Gaps where bugs or animals get in
  • Siding that needs painting every few years

Not sure? We’ll take a look for free and tell you honestly what your home needs.

Siding Questions, Answered

How long does new siding take?

Most homes take a few days to a week, depending on the size of the house, how much trim work is involved and the weather. We’ll give you a timeline with your estimate.

Can you match my existing siding after storm damage?

We do our best to find the same or a very close product and color. If an exact match isn’t available, we’ll show you options before any work starts.

Is insulated vinyl worth it?

Insulated vinyl looks straighter on the wall, resists dents better and adds some insulation. It costs more than standard vinyl, so we’ll help you decide if it makes sense for your home.

Do you replace rotted wood under the siding?

Yes. When the old siding comes off, we check the sheathing and replace any rotted sections before the house wrap and new siding go on.

Can you replace siding on just one side of the house?

Yes, if the damage is limited to one wall and we can match your existing siding closely enough. If a good match isn’t possible, we’ll show you options, such as residing a full wall so the color difference isn’t noticeable.

Will new siding make my home more energy efficient?

New siding with a proper house wrap and sealed seams cuts drafts, and insulated vinyl adds a layer of foam on top of that. Many homeowners notice a more even temperature inside, especially in rooms on windy sides of the house.
