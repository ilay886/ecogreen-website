Privacy Policy

Effective November 3, 2026

Who we are

This website is run by EcoGreen Roofing And Exteriors, a roofing and exterior contractor serving Pennsylvania, New Jersey and Delaware (PA HIC #PA209178, NJ HIC #13VH14032800). In this policy, “we” and “us” mean EcoGreen Roofing And Exteriors. You can reach us at info@ecogreenextrior.com or (888) 494-1248.

What we collect

When you request an estimate, we collect what you type into the form: your full name, phone number, email address, ZIP code, the service you need and your project description. We also note which page of our site you sent the form from. Like most websites, our hosting provider may keep basic technical logs, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you visit, to keep the site secure and working.

How we use it

We use your information only to reply to your request, schedule a visit, prepare your estimate, do the work you hire us for and follow up about that work. By sending the form, you agree that we may contact you by phone, text message or email about your estimate. Message and data rates may apply. You can ask us to stop contacting you at any time.

Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with services that help us run this website and our business, such as FormSubmit, which delivers website form requests to our email inbox, our email and phone providers, and our website host. We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights or the safety of others.

Third-party services

Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts and include links to our Google Business Profile. When your browser connects to these services, they may receive your IP address under their own privacy policies. We do not use advertising cookies or tracking pixels on this website.

Cookies and your choices

We do not use advertising or tracking cookies. When you accept or decline the cookie notice, or change the accessibility settings, your choice is saved only in your own browser so you are not asked again. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

How long we keep it

We keep estimate requests and customer records for as long as we need them to serve you, handle warranty questions and meet our legal, tax and licensing obligations. After that, we delete them or keep them only in a form that no longer identifies you.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email info@ecogreenextrior.com or call (888) 494-1248, and we will respond within a reasonable time. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information. No website or email system is perfectly secure, so please do not send sensitive details such as payment card or Social Security numbers through our form.

Children

This website is meant for adults and homeowners. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
