Local Roofing Experts in Middletown, DE

Whether your Middletown home is brand new or an older house near Main Street, we can help. Farmland around town has turned into big subdivisions, while the older homes still line the downtown streets.

In the newer neighborhoods, open land lets the wind tug at shingles. Common calls are lifted shingles, ridge caps that blew off and small leaks around vent pipes. We fix these quickly and help with storm claims. Builder gutters are often too small, so we upgrade to larger seamless gutters with leaf guards and refasten loose soffit and fascia. Buying or selling? We do roof inspections too.

Downtown, the older homes are usually ready for a full new roof. We'll build one that lasts in architectural shingles, or metal where it suits the house, and redo low porch roofs properly. Wood siding can be repaired or upgraded to fiber cement, and we choose replacement windows that respect each home's character. Any house might enjoy a new storm door. We also soft-wash roofs and siding.

Licensed and insured, EcoGreen Roofing And Exteriors relies on a team with 25+ years of experience. Estimates are always free and no-pressure.
